    Tried and dropped (0)

    Nothing dropped yet. Turn on “Mark fictions you tried and dropped” above, then use the bookmark button on any fiction card on Royal Road.

      Tag colours

      Give a tag a colour and it carries it everywhere it appears, on the fiction lists and on a fiction’s own page. Tags you do not pick are left as Royal Road styles them.

      The home page writes its tags as plain chips with no tag id on them, so they are matched by name instead. A colour added before the extension had seen Royal Road’s tag list has no name recorded yet; it picks one up the next time you open a fiction list.
